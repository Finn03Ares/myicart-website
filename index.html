Why iCart

Eat well for less, without the hassle.

Every feature has to do at least one of three things.

Spend less

Dinners are planned around what's actually on special this week. iCart compares true unit prices ($/kg, $/L), not just the sticker.

Save time

Pick four or five weeknight dinners, most under 35 minutes. The ingredients go straight onto one list, grouped by store.

Waste less

Meal plans

Pick dinners, not a spreadsheet.

  1. Set your budget

    Tell us your household size, preferred stores and what you'd like to spend. We only ask what we need.

  2. Choose your dinners

    Browse 8–12 recommended dinners built on this week's specials. Pick the four or five you like.

  3. Get one list

    Ingredients are combined, sorted by Coles, Woolworths and ALDI, and checked against your pantry.

  4. Shop together

    Share the list with your household. Tick items off in the aisle and everyone sees it straight away.

Questions

Good to know

When is iCart launching?

iCart is in private testing now. We're launching in Brisbane first and will email everyone on the list before it opens.

Which supermarkets does it cover?

The plan is Coles, Woolworths and ALDI to start. iCart isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any supermarket.

How much will it cost?

The weekly email will be free. A paid plan with more personalisation is planned; pricing isn't final yet, and people on the early-access list will hear about founding-member pricing first.

How much will I save?

It depends on your household, your stores and the week's specials, so we won't promise a number. iCart only shows savings it can back up with a real price comparison.

Can my household share one list?

Yes. The shopping list is shared, so everyone sees items added or ticked off as they happen.
