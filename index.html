Eat well for less, without the hassle.

Spend less

Meals are built around what's on special this week. Prices are compared per kilo or litre, so a bigger pack that's actually worse value doesn't slip through.

Save time

Plan the week's dinners, the school-lunch baking and the weekend cake in one go. Everything you need goes onto one list.

Waste less

Meals share ingredients across the week, so half a bunch of coriander doesn't end up in the bin. Tell iCart what's in the pantry and it won't add it again.

How it works

  1. Set a budget

    Tell us how many you're feeding, what you want to spend and where you like to shop.

  2. Pick your meals

    Choose from dinners, lunches and baking that suit this week's specials. Keep the ones you like.

  3. Get the list

    Ingredients are combined, sorted by your selected stores, and checked against your pantry.

  4. Shop together

    Share the list with your household. When someone ticks something off, everyone sees it.

Have a go

Pick a few meals and see the shopping list put itself together. Ingredients that turn up in more than one meal are bought once, so you're not paying for a second pack of butter you don't need.

    About the prices

    A cheap total is no use if it's wrong. So iCart tells you what it doesn't know.

    • Every price shows when it was last checked.
    • If a price can't be confirmed, the item is marked unpriced. We don't guess.
    • If we're not sure two products match, you choose. Nothing gets swapped without asking.
    • Specials that have ended come off the list.

    Questions

    When does it launch?

    We're testing it with a few QLD households now. Everyone on the list hears from us before it opens.

    What will it cost?

    The Wednesday email is free. A paid plan with more options will come later. We haven't set the price yet, and people on the list will hear about it first.

    How much will I save?

    It depends on what you buy and when, so we won't put a number on it. When iCart shows a saving, it's based on a real price comparison.

    Can the whole household use the same list?

    Yes. Everyone shares one list and sees changes as they happen.
