What this covers

This policy covers this website and the iCart early-access list. iCart is not yet publicly available; when it is, this policy will be updated to cover the app.

What we collect

To tell you when iCart launches, to decide which suburbs to launch in first, and to send occasional updates about the product. We'll only email you about iCart.

Who we share it with

Your details are stored with the service we use to run the sign-up form and send email. We don't share them with anyone else for marketing.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello.icartau@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions or complaints: hello.icartau@gmail.com. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
